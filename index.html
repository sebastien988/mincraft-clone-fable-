Controls
  • WASD move · Space jump · Shift sneak
  • Ctrl or hold W twice to sprint
  • Creative: F or double-tap Space to fly · Space/Shift to rise and fall
  • Left click breaks (hold to mine in survival) · right click places · middle click picks
  • 1–9 or scroll to pick a slot · E opens the block list (your inventory in survival)
  • F3 debug overlay · Esc pause
